# Bianca Voice Profile

## Evidence boundary

- **Established Canon:** Bianca appears in `D1_CLASSROOM_RECESS_BIANCA` and invites Audere
  to help with classroom decoration.
- **Established Canon:** she notices the startle, apologizes, explains the practical task,
  asks once, waits, and explicitly leaves Audere room to decline.
- **Design Intent:** Bianca is an ordinary classmate interaction, not a rescuer or a person
  who immediately understands Audere's inner life.
- **Unresolved:** age, history, wider friendships, personal stakes, portrait, final art, and
  behavior outside this short classroom beat.

## Current voice

- Friendly but measured; warmth comes from consideration, not high energy.
- Uses short practical statements before the invitation.
- Treats Audere as a classmate who can choose, not as someone fragile who must be managed.
- If Audere hesitates, Bianca reduces pressure once and then gives space.
- Does not diagnose, over-reassure, demand an explanation, or promise to “save” Audere.

## Established line pattern

```text
gọi tên
→ nhận ra phản ứng và xin lỗi
→ nêu việc cụ thể
→ mời với phạm vi nhỏ
→ mở đường lui
```

This pattern is evidence for the current beat, not a mandatory template for every Bianca scene.
